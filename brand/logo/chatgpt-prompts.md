# Spotto logo — ChatGPT prompts

## Spotto: spot it, save it, go there

**What it is**
Spotto is a travel app that makes it effortless to find great places and plan a trip around them, anywhere in the world. It connects the people who travel with the local businesses they want to discover.

**The problem it solves**
Travel inspiration is scattered: saved reels, screenshots, friends' tips, a dozen open map tabs. People see a beautiful café or viewpoint in a video and never find it again. Spotto collects all of that inspiration and turns it into real places on a map, then into a plan.

**How a traveller uses it**
- **Spot:** paste any Instagram, TikTok or YouTube travel reel into the **Inspiration Board**. Spotto watches it, works out which real places appear in it, and saves them automatically.
- **Discover:** explore a live map of cafés, restaurants, stays, tours, activities and hidden gems nearby, with the day's weather, filters, and photos and reviews for every place.
- **Save:** keep favourite places in one list. Spotto sends a **nearby alert** when the traveller is close to one, so they never walk past a spot they wanted to visit.
- **Plan:** build a day-by-day trip, plan together with friends in a group, and book experiences directly in the app.
- **Enjoy:** complete small discovery quests, and unlock local deals by showing a QR code at the venue.

**For local businesses**
Cafés, restaurants, stays, tour operators and activity providers get a **vendor portal** where they can:
- manage their listing, photos, opening hours and prices;
- publish deals;
- handle bookings;
- scan travellers' QR codes to redeem offers.

This puts their business in front of travellers at the moment those travellers are looking for somewhere to go.

**Behind the scenes**
A Spotto team uses an **admin command center** to approve and moderate places and businesses, keeping the listings trustworthy.

**Who it's for**
Curious, independent travellers (solo, couples and friend groups) who love finding places that feel special rather than touristy, and who get their travel ideas from social media.

**Personality**
- **Fun and a little quirky.** The name "Spotto" sounds like a cheerful "spot it!"
- **Premium and calm.** Uncluttered, confident and trustworthy, never loud or cheap.
- **Adventurous and curious.** It celebrates the joy of discovering somewhere new.

**Look and feel ("Sunny Postcard")**
Warm and sunny, like a holiday postcard. Colours:
- Cream background `#FBF6ED`
- Deep teal ink `#103D3A`
- Ocean teal `#0B6F86`
- Lagoon `#0E8C8A`
- A touch of sun yellow `#F6B81E`, for highlights only

**For the logo, avoid:** a plain map pin, globe, airplane, suitcase or compass; heavy black; gradients; clip art; anything tied to one country or city.

## 10 prompts

Put this line at the end of every prompt:

> *Flat vector logo, clean geometric shapes, 2–3 colours from: deep teal #103D3A, ocean teal #0B6F86, sun yellow #F6B81E, on a cream #FBF6ED background. Centred, plenty of empty space, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no extra text. Must still read clearly as a tiny 32 px app icon.*

### Playful character
1. **The Spotter.** A small, round explorer creature, a simple blob shape with one big curious eye that works as the "spot", peeking up as if it has just found something great. Minimal smooth shapes, a cheeky but calm expression. The wordmark "Spotto" sits beside it in a rounded, friendly sans-serif.
2. **Lookout bird.** A round little bird with a single large eye, tilting its head as if it has spotted something. Its tail curves into a soft path line. Simple, bold and charming, like a premium consumer-app mascot.
3. **Peekaboo face.** A minimal face peeking over a horizon line, made of only 4–5 shapes: two wide eyes, one of them a sun-yellow dot. It captures the delight of discovering a new place. Playful, adventurous, premium.
4. **Happy backpack buddy.** A rounded backpack drawn front-on as a friendly face: the pocket is a smile and a round sun-yellow badge is the eye. Very simple geometry that works as an app icon.

### Bold abstract symbol
5. **Spot & path.** One continuous, smooth path line that loops into an abstract letter "S" and ends in a sun-yellow dot: the place you're heading to. Balanced, calm, premium.
6. **Binocular spots.** Two overlapping circles, like binocular lenses, where the overlap forms a small lens shape in sun yellow: the moment you "spot" something. Minimal and premium, like a modern travel brand.
7. **Saved-place stack.** Three rounded cards fanned like saved places or postcards. A small sun-yellow dot sits on the top card, and together the cards form a subtle "S". Feels like a premium travel-planning app icon.

### Badge / stamp
8. **Passport stamp.** A rounded rectangular passport-stamp shape with a slightly imperfect inked edge, containing a minimal sun-over-horizon icon and "SPOTTO" in small, wide-spaced capitals. Retro, warm, simple enough to read small.
9. **Explorer seal.** A circular badge with "SPOTTO · SPOT IT · SAVE IT · GO" around the ring. In the centre is a minimal sun rising behind a stylised horizon of hills and buildings. Premium, calm, timeless.
10. **Luggage-tag badge.** A rounded luggage-tag shape with a small hole at the top, containing a bold, quirky "S" whose lower end finishes in a sun-yellow dot. Adventurous and fun, but tidy and premium.

**Tips for ChatGPT:**
- Ask for **"3 variations, same prompt"**.
- Follow up with **"make it simpler, fewer shapes"** if a result is too detailed.
- Once you like one, ask for **"the same logo as a solid-colour app icon"**.
