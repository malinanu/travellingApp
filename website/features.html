One app, the whole island.

Everything below ships free in the Spotto app. No premium traveller tier, no ads pretending to be recommendations.

A map with taste

Open the map and see only places worth your time: reviewed by locals, tagged by vibe, and refreshed when a spot slips.

  • 120+ hand-picked spots across the island
  • Filter by vibe: surf, food, waterfalls, wildlife
  • Honest notes on price, crowds and timing

An AI guide that knows the back roads

Type a question the way you'd ask a friend. The guide answers from Spotto's local knowledge, not a generic web search.

  • "Quiet beach with shade near Tangalle?"
  • Answers link straight to real map pins
  • Works in English, French, German and more

Deals and quests that pay for lunch

Partner spots run real offers for Spotto travellers. Claim in the app, show the code, and stamp your quest book while you're at it.

  • Claim a deal in two taps, redeem with a QR code
  • Quests turn regions into treasure hunts
  • Stamps unlock rewards at partner spots

Alerts tuned to your favourite breaks

Follow the breaks you surf and Spotto watches the swell, wind and tide so you can sleep until it matters.

  • Morning report for Arugam Bay, Weligama and more
  • A push the evening before it turns on
  • Crowd notes from surfers already in the water

A trip planner your crew will actually open

Drag spots into days, share one link with the group, and let the map figure out a sane driving order.

  • Day-by-day boards built from map pins
  • Share with friends, edit together
  • Offline copies for the hill-country dead zones

Free for travellers. Always.
