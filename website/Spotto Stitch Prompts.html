How to use these

Stitch makes one screen per prompt and does not remember the style between screens, so every page prompt starts with the style block.

  1. Start a Stitch project in web / desktop mode.
  2. Choose a concept in the bar above, then press Copy on a page prompt. The matching style block is added to the top automatically.
  3. Attach reference images: brand/logo/png/spotto-horizontal.png, the three app screens in website/assets/img (app-map.png, app-guide.png, app-deal.png) and hero-arugam.jpg.
  4. Generate Home first. When it looks right, generate the other six pages, then ask for the mobile version of each at 390 px wide.
  5. Swap in the real logo and photos by hand, then export to Figma or code.

Style block A · Pocket Local

Light, photographic, bento grid, frosted pill nav

Design a modern, premium, fully responsive marketing website for Spotto, a free Sri Lanka travel app with a companion portal for local businesses. Mood: sunny holiday postcard, calm, premium, a little quirky.

COLOURS (use exactly): page background cream #FBF6ED; cards white #FFFFFF; alternate sections sand #F3ECDD; headlines and dark sections ink #103D3A; body copy #3A5A57; primary buttons and links ocean teal #0B6F86; hover and secondary lagoon #0E8C8A; chips and soft fills #DDEDEF and #BDE4E0; one dark feature section in deep teal #0B4A54; sun gold #F6B81E only as a highlight (badges, stars, small fills, a hand-drawn underline swoosh, or text on dark ink, never gold text on cream); borders #E7E1D4. No purple, no neon, no default blue.

TYPE: bold modern geometric sans for headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans or Bricolage Grotesque), very large and tightly tracked (hero 80 to 96px), Nunito 17 to 18px for body.

STYLE: modern 2026 editorial. Oversized type, generous whitespace (120px between sections), full-bleed cinematic golden-hour photography with a soft ink overlay, bento-grid feature layouts with 24 to 32px rounded cards, a floating frosted-glass pill navigation bar, pill buttons, soft layered shadows tinted with ink (rgba 16,61,58,0.12), 1px borders, subtle film grain, large phone mockups overlapping section edges, a scrolling strip of place names, scroll-reveal motion.

BRAND MOTIFS: a half sun rising over three rounded horizon lines (the Spotto logo) used as section dividers; a passport-stamp frame (double rounded rectangle) for badges. Logo: top-left placeholder "Spotto" beside a half-sun-over-waves icon.

IMAGERY: warm golden-hour Sri Lanka photography (surfer at Arugam Bay, blue train at Ella, Sigiriya rock, tea hills, street food, local cafe owners) and real app screenshots inside iPhone frames.

ACCESSIBILITY: WCAG AA contrast, 48px touch targets, visible focus rings. Tone: warm, concise, traveller to traveller.

Style block B · Eight Wonders Atlas

Dark, cinematic, brand colours, rising-sun hero

Design a modern, immersive, fully responsive marketing website for Spotto, a free Sri Lanka travel app with a companion portal for local businesses. Concept: an atlas of Sri Lanka's eight wonders at golden hour, with the Spotto logo's rising half sun as the central image.

Refinement prompts

Paste these into Stitch after a screen is generated.

Make the hero bolder and more cinematic: larger headline, deeper overlay, and a phone mockup that overlaps the next section.

Convert this screen to a 390px mobile layout: single column, bottom-aligned CTA, collapsible nav.

Tighten the colour usage: remove any colour not in the palette and use sun gold only as a highlight.

Increase text contrast to WCAG AA and make all touch targets at least 48px.

Replace stock-looking imagery with warm golden-hour Sri Lanka photography and real app screenshots in iPhone frames.

Add a subtle scroll-reveal and hover motion to cards, and a marquee strip of place names.

What we deliver to each customer

These are the promises the website can make today.

To travellers

  • Find the real places fast. One map with photos, hours and honest vibe ratings.
  • Plan without juggling apps. Ask the AI guide, paste a reel, or tap an itinerary and it becomes a trip.
  • Save money and have fun. Deals redeemed with a QR, and a daily quest.
  • Travel with confidence. Weather scored for what they came to do, and alerts near saved places.
  • Travel together. Group code and live locations.
  • Free. The traveller app costs nothing.

To local businesses

  • Get found by nearby travellers. A verified listing on the map the app uses.
  • Turn visits into customers. Offers, bookings and quest sponsorship.
  • Run it from a phone. Scan a code and it validates in one tap.
  • Look trustworthy. A person checks every listing before it goes live.
  • Stay in control. Hours, menu, photos, team roles and reports in one place.
  • Start free. The Starter listing is live and costs nothing.

Keep off the website for now

  • Paid billing, free trials, yearly pricing, boosts. There is no payment processor.
  • Flash sales pushed to travellers, stamp cards, "Nudge all". Not built.
  • Live analytics charts. Dashboard numbers are demo data. Say "reports" instead.
  • Other languages, offline mode, phone sign-in. Not built.
  • Joining a group by scanning a QR. Group codes exist, joining from a code or link is unfinished.
  • Swell or tide surf forecasts. Weather uses wind and rain only.
  • Testimonials and partner or traveller counts. None are real yet.

Website assets still to deliver

  • Real photography and clean app screenshots to replace the generated images.
  • A pricing decision. Only the free Starter listing is real.
  • Store links, contact email and phone, and a backend for the contact form.
  • Privacy and Terms pages. The vendor portal already has them.
  • The real logo files from brand/logo/, imported by hand.

Decisions to make before launch

  1. PricingThe marketing page shows $49, the vendor portal shows $29 and $99, and the database has only Free and Premium. Pick one, or launch with free Starter only.
  2. PhotographyCommission or shoot real photos of the eight wonders and local businesses.
  3. Contact and store linksProvide the support email, phone and app store URLs.
  4. "1,000+ places"The seed file has 1,081 entries. Confirm against production before using the number.
